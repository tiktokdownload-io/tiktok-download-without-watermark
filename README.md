# TikTok Download Without Watermark

A simple guide to saving TikTok videos in HD without the watermark, on any device, for free.

## How to download a TikTok video without watermark

1. Open the TikTok app or website and find the video you want to save.
2. Tap Share, then Copy link.
3. Open [TikTokDownload.io](https://tiktokdownload.io/) in your browser.
4. Paste the link into the input box and press Download.
5. Choose MP4 (no watermark, HD) or MP3 (audio only) and save the file.

## Works on every device

* Android: use Chrome or any browser, files go to your Downloads folder.
* iPhone and iPad: use Safari, then open the Files app to save the video to Photos.
* Windows, Mac and Linux: works in any modern browser with no software to install.
